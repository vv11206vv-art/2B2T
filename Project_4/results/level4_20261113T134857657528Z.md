## Historical evidence

Two rolling seven-day recorded-rental proxy tests; training uses only rentals returned and booked strictly before each cutoff. Ten-day left burn-in; targets end at least 14 days before the horizon. Zero cells are included. Historical booking amendments, cancellations, rejected demand and fleet constraints are unavailable. This is not a validated backtest of latent unconstrained demand. Completed-only training still has right-edge completion bias. Observed maximum length 10 days does not establish a bound on missing rentals. Four interior weekly blocks are too few to validate probabilities or tails.
